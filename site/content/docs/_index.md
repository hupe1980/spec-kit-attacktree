AttackTree adds attack trees to [Spec Kit](https://github.com/github/spec-kit)'s Spec-Driven Development workflow. New here? Read [What is AttackTree?](@/docs/introduction.md), then follow the [quickstart](@/docs/quickstart.md). The rest is organised by what you need: concepts explain how and why it works, guides solve a task, and the reference lists every command, field, and setting.
